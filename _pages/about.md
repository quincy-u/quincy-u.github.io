I am a CS PhD student at **UIUC**, supervised by [Prof. Shenlong Wang](https://shenlong.web.illinois.edu/). My research focuses on **robot learning**.

Before this, I completed my CS bachelor's at the **University of Toronto**, supervised by [Prof. Animesh Garg](https://animesh.garg.tech/) (now at Georgia Tech). I have also worked closely with [Prof. Ken Goldberg](https://goldberg.berkeley.edu/) at UC Berkeley and [Prof. Xiaolong Wang](https://xiaolonw.github.io/) at UC San Diego.

My dream is to make intelligent robots integrated into our daily lives.
